About

Engineering the future of hydrogen storage

I am a First Class Honours Mechanical Engineering graduate (GPA: 3.8/4.0) and a Visiting Researcher at Khalifa University, with a strong interest in composite materials, hydrogen storage systems, advanced manufacturing, and marine engineering.

My current research focuses on Type IV Composite Overwrapped Pressure Vessels (COPVs), laminate optimization, and Automated Fibre Placement (AFP) for lightweight hydrogen storage applications. Through this work, I have developed experience in composite design, engineering analysis, CAD, Python, and research-driven problem solving.

Previously, I completed an internship at Archirodon Construction, where I gained hands-on experience in marine maintenance, fabrication, heavy equipment repair, and workshop operations — strengthening my understanding of engineering applications across the marine and construction industries.

Alongside my technical work, I currently serve as Chairperson of the IMechE Student Chapter at the University of Greater Manchester, leading initiatives that connect students with industry, promote professional development, and encourage engineering outreach.
